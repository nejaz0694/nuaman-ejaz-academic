Research

Water science across scales.

My work connects climate forcing, hydrological processes, remote sensing, and data-driven models to understand water availability, extremes, and resilience in complex basins.

01
Hydroclimate

Drought & extremes

Multi-scale meteorological and hydrological drought, propagation, intensity–duration–frequency behaviour, and large-scale climate controls.

02
Cryosphere

Snow, glaciers & runoff

Process-based representation of snow- and glacier-influenced catchments, seasonal flow generation, peak behaviour, and future streamflow.

03
Earth observation

Remote sensing & groundwater

GRACE/GRACE-FO, MODIS, Landsat, Sentinel, reanalysis, and GIS for water storage, drought, land-use, and environmental monitoring.

04
Modelling

Hydrological simulation

DDD, SWAT, HEC-HMS, HEC-RAS, MODFLOW, conceptual modelling, calibration, validation, and benchmark-based evaluation.

05
Climate futures

CMIP6 projections

Bias-corrected climate forcing, model screening, uncertainty-aware projections, changing high flows, drought, and hydroclimatic reorganization.

06
Data science

ML for Earth systems

Python, Google Earth Engine, Random Forest, XGBoost, SVM, ANN/LSTM, statistical learning, wavelets, and geospatial analysis.
