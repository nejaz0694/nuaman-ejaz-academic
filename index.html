About

Connecting climate, cryosphere and streamflow.

I am a PhD researcher in the Department of Hydraulic Engineering at Tsinghua University, working at the intersection of hydrology, climate science, Earth observation and data-driven modelling.

My research focuses on the Upper Indus River system, where snow, glaciers, monsoon rainfall and westerly disturbances interact to create complex streamflow and drought responses.

Current work

Research projects.

01

PhD · Upper Indus

Integrated glacio-hydrological modelling of streamflow variability under a changing climate

Process understanding, source partitioning, drought propagation, high-flow behaviour and future climate impacts across contrasting Indus headwater catchments.

02

Daily modelling

When predictive skill and hydrological process realism diverge

Comparison of DDD, forcing-only and hydrologically informed LSTMs with climatology and persistence benchmarks across five mountain catchments.

03

Groundwater · Earth observation

Groundwater dynamics and agricultural water use

GRACE-based storage assessment, groundwater modelling and remote-sensing analysis of cropping patterns and irrigation demand.
